demo_strokes.jsonl holds single glide strokes adapted (re-projected onto a Pixel-8 key geometry by
tools/data/human/build.py, like tools/data/human/splits/v1) from two datasets; their licences apply.

ids futo/...: swipe.futo.org dataset, Copyright (c) 2025 FUTO, MIT License.
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/futo-org/swipe.futo.org
ids hws/...: How-We-Swipe Shape-writing Dataset, Copyright (c) 2021 L. A. Leiva, S. Kim, W. Cui, X. Bi,
  A. Oulasvirta, MIT License. https://osf.io/sj67f/

The MIT permission notice is reproduced in tools/data/human/splits/v1/ATTRIBUTION.txt.

The demo's tapped words (tools/media/demo_gif.sh, steps tap=...) are taken from tools/data/human/splits/v1, not
from this file: ids tsi/... from the Tap Typing with Touch Sensing Images (TSI) Dataset, Google, CC BY 4.0, and
ids vtap/... from A Dataset of Noisy Typing on QWERTY Keyboards, K. Vertanen and P. O. Kristensson, CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); full credits and changes in tools/data/human/splits/v1/ATTRIBUTION.txt.
