geometry, segmented into words and split by participant; see docs/eval-data.md). Their licences apply to the
corresponding files.

swipe_hws.*.jsonl.gz
  How-We-Swipe Shape-writing Dataset, Copyright (c) 2021 L. A. Leiva, S. Kim, W. Cui, X. Bi, A. Oulasvirta,
  MIT License (same permission notice as above). https://osf.io/sj67f/
  L. A. Leiva et al. "How We Swipe: A Large-scale Shape-writing Dataset and Empirical Findings." MobileHCI 2021.

tap_tsi.*.jsonl.gz, tap-derived items with src "tsi" in text_typos.*.jsonl.gz
  Tap Typing with Touch Sensing Images (TSI) Dataset, Google, CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  https://github.com/google-research-datasets/tap-typing-with-touch-sensing-images
  P. Lertvittayakumjorn, S. Cai, B. Dou, C. Ho, S. Zhai. "Can Capacitive Touch Images Enhance Mobile Keyboard
  Decoding?" UIST 2024. Changes: word segmentation, first-pass reconstruction, coordinate re-projection,
  heatmaps/LM scores dropped.

tap_vtap.*.jsonl.gz, tap-derived items with src "vtap" in text_typos.*.jsonl.gz
  A Dataset of Noisy Typing on QWERTY Keyboards, K. Vertanen and P. O. Kristensson, CC BY 4.0.
  https://osf.io/5xwng/  (IUI 2023). Data originally from Vertanen et al. "VelociTap", CHI 2015.
  Changes: phone subset (vt_exp2 space condition, vt_exp3 normal keyboard), touch-down positions re-projected,
  synthetic word-terminating space tap added.

text_wikipedia.*.jsonl.gz
